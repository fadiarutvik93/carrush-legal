Car Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026. Published by Nivik Technologies. Contact: fadiarutvik93@gmail.com

Car Rush ("the game") is a free 3D endless-driving game for Android. This page explains what information the game and the services it uses handle. In short: the game has no accounts, and Nivik Technologies runs no servers that store your data. Your progress stays on your phone.

1. What stays on your phone

Your coins, gems, cars, upgrades, missions, settings, display name, scores and a random player ID are saved on your device only. We never receive them. You can delete all of it at any time with Settings > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game. If you use Android's backup, Google may back up this saved data to your Google account under Google's own terms.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and, after some game-overs, an interstitial ad, using Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: your device's IP address (from which a general location may be estimated), ad interactions such as taps and views, diagnostic information about the app and SDK, and the Android advertising ID and app-set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in Android's Settings (Google > Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players who buy the Ad-Free item or the VIP pass see no ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; the game only learns whether a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item.

4. Multiplayer

When you create or join a room, phones connect directly to each other (WebRTC). A free public signalling service (PeerJS) helps the phones find each other. As a result, your IP address is shared with the other players in the room and with that signalling service for the length of the match. The other players also see your display name, car, level and race results, and the preset quick-chat messages you send. We do not keep a record of rooms or matches. Hot-Seat mode needs no network.

5. Daily reminders

If you allow it, the game schedules reminder notifications on your phone (around 6:30 pm). They are created and delivered by your phone; nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off in Settings > Daily reminders or in Android's notification settings.

6. Children

Car Rush is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents who believe a child has used the game can contact us at the address above.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our own servers, there is normally nothing for us to delete or export. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights over data processed by Google or others; you can exercise them through Google's tools or by contacting us, and we will help where we can.

8. Security

Network traffic to ad and purchase services is encrypted. Game data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

9. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date.
