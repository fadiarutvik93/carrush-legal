Car Rush - Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Published by Nivik Technologies. Contact: fadiarutvik93@gmail.com

Car Rush ("the game") is a free 3D endless-driving game for Android. This page explains what information the game and the services it uses handle. In short: the game has no sign-up and no login, and your progress stays on your phone. The only thing we store online is your optional entry on the worldwide leaderboard (section 5).

1. What stays on your phone

Your coins, gems, cars, upgrades, missions, settings, display name, scores and a random player ID are saved on your device only. The same goes for the first name, last name and age you may enter on the welcome screen: they stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else; only the short racer name made from them (for example "Rutvik S.", which shows just the first letter of your last name) can appear on the online leaderboard, and only if you are 13 or older. We never receive them. You can delete all of it at any time with Settings > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game. If you use Android's backup, Google may back up this saved data to your Google account under Google's own terms.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads and, after some game-overs, an interstitial ad, using Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: your device's IP address (from which a general location may be estimated), ad interactions such as taps and views, diagnostic information about the app and SDK, and the Android advertising ID and app-set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in Android's Settings (Google > Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Players who buy the Ad-Free item or the VIP pass see no ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; the game only learns whether a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item.

4. Multiplayer

When you create or join a room, phones connect directly to each other (WebRTC). A free public signalling service (PeerJS) helps the phones find each other. As a result, your IP address is shared with the other players in the room and with that signalling service for the length of the match. The other players also see your display name, car, level and race results, and the preset quick-chat messages you send. We do not keep a record of rooms or matches. Hot-Seat mode needs no network.

5. Online leaderboard (Google Firebase)

The worldwide leaderboard is optional and you can switch it off at any time in Settings > Online ranking. When it is on, the game creates an anonymous Firebase (Google) user for your phone, with a random ID and no email, phone number or other personal details, and stores in a Firebase Realtime Database: your racer name (chosen by you; please do not use your real name), your best results (longest run, most coins in a run, best combo, career distance, this week's distance), your player level and the car you use, and the time of the update. Other players can see this information on the leaderboard. Firebase also receives your phone's IP address when the game connects, under Google's Firebase privacy terms. We use this data only to show the leaderboard. Switching off Online ranking deletes your entries from the leaderboard right away; you can also write to us to have them removed.

6. Daily reminders

If you allow it, the game schedules reminder notifications on your phone (around 6:30 pm). They are created and delivered by your phone; nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off in Settings > Daily reminders or in Android's notification settings.

7. Children

Car Rush is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The welcome screen asks for your age; if you enter an age under 13, the game keeps you off the online leaderboard (nothing is sent to it) and asks Google AdMob to treat ads as child-directed and family friendly. Parents who believe a child has used the game can contact us at the address above.

8. Your choices and rights

Apart from the optional leaderboard entry described in section 5 (which you can delete yourself in Settings), we hold no personal data about you, so there is normally nothing for us to delete or export. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights over data processed by Google or others; you can exercise them through Google's tools or by contacting us, and we will help where we can.

9. Security

Network traffic to ad, purchase and leaderboard services is encrypted. Game data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date.
